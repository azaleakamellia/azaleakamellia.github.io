Prologue

My work exists at the intersection of rigorous academic research and practical climate risk assessment. I am a geospatial scientist specializing in satellite precipitation calibration, Standardized Precipitation Index (SPI) modeling, and machine learning bias correction for hydroclimatic applications. Currently completing my MPhil at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, I operate as a scholar-practitioner — committed to peer-reviewed quality, open knowledge, and ethical commerce. This compendium serves as a living catalogue of my research, tools, field notes, and published works.
