Research Interests

Hydrometeorology & Drought Monitoring

Investigating how satellite-derived precipitation data can be corrected and validated to better capture drought conditions — particularly in tropical regions where ground observations are sparse.

MPhil Research

Remote Sensing & Satellite Data

Working with products like CHIRPS, PERSIANN, and IMERG to extract meaningful environmental signals. Focused on bias correction methodologies for improving data reliability over Borneo.

Core Expertise

Machine Learning on Geospatial Data

Applying ML approaches to environmental datasets — from classification of land cover change to regression models for climate indices. Bridging the gap between data science and earth observation.
