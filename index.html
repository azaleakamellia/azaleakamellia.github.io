Preface: Operating Principles

My work exists at the intersection of rigorous academic research and practical climate risk assessment. Guided by the principles of Sadaqah Jariyah (beneficial, enduring knowledge) and Tijarah & Ihsan (fair, excellent commerce), I maintain a strict dual-license framework. Baseline methodologies, tutorials, and code are openly shared for students and researchers. Polished, heavy-lifting tools and bespoke reports are reserved for corporate clients (ESG auditors, sustainability directors) via commercial licensing. Intellectual property is established via MyIPO (Malaysia), intentionally avoiding patents in favor of open, ethical stewardship.

Chapter I: Tools & Implementations

  • Asset-Level Physical Climate & Drought Risk Profiling Pipeline. An automated Python/Google Earth Engine workflow for corporate TCFD and ISSB (IFRS S2) reporting. Ingests asset coordinates, retrieves ERA5-Land/CHIRPS/CMIP6 data, applies ML bias correction (XGBoost/Random Forest) against local station data, and outputs standardized SPI metrics and drought return periods.
