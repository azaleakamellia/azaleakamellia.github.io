My work exists at the intersection of rigorous academic research and practical climate risk assessment. Guided by the principles of Sadaqah Jariyah (beneficial, enduring knowledge) and Tijarah & Ihsan (fair, excellent commerce), I maintain a strict dual-license framework. Baseline methodologies, tutorials, and code are openly shared for students and researchers. Polished, heavy-lifting tools and bespoke reports are reserved for corporate clients via commercial licensing. Intellectual property is established via MyIPO (Malaysia), intentionally avoiding patents in favor of open, ethical stewardship.
