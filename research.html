  • Satellite Precipitation Estimates for Drought Monitoring in Sarawak: A Systematic Review
    MPhil Research Project · Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (2024–2026)
    In Progress – Proposal Revision Stage

    A comprehensive systematic literature review utilizing the ROSES (Reporting Standards for Systematic Evidence Syntheses) framework to evaluate bias correction methodologies for satellite precipitation products in tropical regions. The review focuses on the applicability of various statistical and machine learning approaches for improving drought monitoring accuracy in data-sparse regions of Sarawak, Malaysia.

    Key Objectives:
    • Evaluate performance of CHIRPS, TRMM, and GPM products in Borneo
    • Assess bias correction techniques (quantile mapping, machine learning)
    • Develop framework for SPI calculation in tropical monsoon climates

  • Machine Learning Approaches for Satellite Precipitation Bias Correction
    Methodological Development · Ongoing Research (2025–Present)
    In Progress

    Development and comparison of machine learning algorithms (XGBoost, Random Forest, Neural Networks) for bias correction of satellite precipitation estimates against ground-based rain gauge observations. The research investigates the trade-offs between model complexity, interpretability, and generalizability across different climatic zones.

    Methodology:
    • Feature engineering from ERA5-Land reanalysis data
    • Cross-validation across temporal and spatial scales
    • Uncertainty quantification using ensemble methods

  • Operationalizing TCFD Recommendations for Asset-Level Climate Risk
    Framework Development · Independent Research (2025)
    Completed

    Development of a standardized framework for translating Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) and IFRS S2 requirements into actionable, asset-level physical climate risk assessments. The framework bridges the gap between high-level corporate disclosure requirements and site-specific climate hazard quantification.

    Components:
    • Hazard identification and materiality screening
    • Scenario analysis integration (CMIP6 SSPs)
    • Financial impact quantification methodologies
    • Integration with existing ESG reporting systems
